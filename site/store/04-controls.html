Every layer switches off on its own.

Six toggles, grouped by consequence. Turn off a single layer when a page misbehaves, instead of disabling the whole extension.

And it tells you when it can’t help

When YouTube stitches an ad into the media stream itself, no browser extension can cleanly remove it. AdPrune detects that case and says so, rather than silently appearing broken.

AdPrune
