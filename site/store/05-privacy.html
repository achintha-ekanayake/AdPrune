Privacy

Two permissions. Zero data collection.

AdPrune originates no network requests of its own. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server to send anything to.

Permissions requested
  • storage Keeps your toggles and counters on your device.
  • declarativeNetRequest Static rules for ad-network hosts. The browser applies them; AdPrune never sees the requests.
Runs only on
  • youtube.com
  • youtube-nocookie.com
  • googlevideo.com
Every other site you visit is out of reach — the extension is not loaded there at all.
Never collected
  • Browsing or watch history
  • Personal or identifying information
  • Location data of any kind
  • Cookies or session tokens
  • Analytics and telemetry
